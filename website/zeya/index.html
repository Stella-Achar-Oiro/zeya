ZEYA

Preparing for trial

Antenatal education for pregnant women, on WhatsApp.

ZEYA is built and run by Evarest Technologies Ltd, a software company incorporated in Kenya.

ZEYA answers questions about pregnancy in English and Kiswahili, using World Health Organization and Kenya Ministry of Health guidelines. It is preparing for a randomised controlled trial in Migori County. It is not yet open to users.

Contact the team View the code

Safety first

Danger signs never wait for AI.

Fixed rules, not AI

Danger signs like bleeding, convulsions or water breaking are detected by fixed rules in English and Kiswahili, never by AI.

Emergency message first

When a danger sign is detected, ZEYA sends an emergency message with health facility contacts before any AI runs.

Education, not diagnosis

ZEYA does not diagnose or treat. It reminds women to attend antenatal care and to see a health worker.

Research

Part of a research study.

ZEYA asks for consent before enrolling anyone in the study. Taking part is voluntary. Researchers analyse conversations under a study number instead of a name or phone number.

Read the ZEYA privacy policy.

Open source

Built in the open.

ZEYA runs on Open Chat Studio, an open-source chatbot platform by Dimagi. ZEYA's code is published under the Apache License 2.0.
